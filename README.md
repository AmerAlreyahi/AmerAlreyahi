I'm a solution architect. I design enterprise solutions end to end: from the
business strategy and the project portfolio, through data and integration, down to the AI systems and platforms that
deliver them. My work follows the TOGAF Architecture Development Method. I'm a Broadcom Certified Partner, focused on ValueOps
(Clarity PPM and value stream management).

## Architecture practice

| Layer | What I do |
|---|---|
| **Business** | Capability maps, value streams and portfolio strategy; Broadcom ValueOps value stream management and Clarity PPM. |
| **Data & applications** | Integration architecture across Clarity PPM, Automic Automation and ITSM; agentic AI and custom MCP servers that extend them. |
| **Technology** | Containerized platforms on Linux and Docker; workflow orchestration with Temporal and Automic. |
| **Governance** | Architecture and AI governance, Generative AI and RAG in regulated environments, roadmaps tied to business value. |
